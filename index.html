About Teerth Realties Owner

Teerth Realties is a Pune-based real estate company with professional roots dating back to 1986 and its formal establishment in 2008. The company focuses on residential and commercial developments, with an emphasis on thoughtful planning, functional design, and creating spaces that respond to evolving customer needs.

Vijay Raundal: Founder and Managing Director

Vijay Raundal is the Founder and Managing Director of Teerth Realties. His entrepreneurial journey began in 1986 and included experience in marble polishing, earthmoving machinery, construction, and government contracting. These experiences helped shape his understanding of project execution and business development. In 2008, he established Teerth Realties, building on his professional experience to pursue opportunities in real estate development.

Abhi Raundal: A Next-Generation Real Estate Leader

Abhi Raundal serves as a Director at Teerth Realties and has been associated with the company since 2017. He holds a master's degree in Real Estate Development from the University of Westminster. His professional responsibilities include planning, procurement, marketing, liaisoning, and development coordination. With a focus on design, business growth, and development processes, Abhi Raundal brings a future-oriented perspective to the company's ongoing work.

The Vision Behind Teerth Realties

The leadership of Vijay Raundal and Abhi Raundal reflects a combination of established entrepreneurial experience and a forward-looking approach to real estate development. Teerth Realties' portfolio includes residential projects such as Teerth Aarohi, Teerth Avila, and Teerth Towers, alongside commercial developments such as Teerth Exchange and Teerth Technospace.

The company's approach centres on planning, design, execution, and understanding how people use residential and commercial spaces. As Pune continues to develop, these considerations remain important for homebuyers, businesses, and communities evaluating real estate opportunities.
